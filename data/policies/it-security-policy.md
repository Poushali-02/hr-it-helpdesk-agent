# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days or immediately after a suspected compromise. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the code repositories and all admin tools. Never share passwords or MFA codes, even with IT staff.

## Phishing and suspicious links
If you click a suspicious link or open a suspicious attachment: disconnect from the network (Wi-Fi off or unplug), do not enter any credentials, and report it to it-helpdesk@northwind.example within 15 minutes. Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail client where possible. IT will reset your credentials and scan the device. Reporting quickly will never be treated as a disciplinary matter.

## VPN
The company VPN must be used on all public and home networks when accessing internal systems. Split tunnelling is disabled. VPN access requires MFA.

## Lost credentials
If you lose access to your account or MFA device, contact the IT helpdesk with your employee ID. IT verifies your identity through your manager before restoring access.
