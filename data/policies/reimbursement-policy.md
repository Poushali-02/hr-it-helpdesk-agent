# Reimbursement Policy

## Internet
Employees who work from home can claim up to INR 1,000 per month towards their home internet bill. A bill or payment receipt for the month is required.

## Travel
Approved business travel is reimbursed at actuals for economy flights, sleeper/3AC trains and local transport. Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere. Travel must be approved by your manager before booking.

## Deadlines
Claims must be submitted in the expense portal within 30 days of the expense, with receipts attached. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment and upgrades beyond the approved class.
