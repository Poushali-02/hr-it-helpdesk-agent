# Remote Work Policy

## Work-from-home days
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. The remaining 2 days are in-office days, agreed with your team so that the whole team is present on the same days. Teams may set stricter rules for on-call or lab-based roles.

## Core hours
Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 11:00 and 16:00 IST on working days for meetings and collaboration.

## Home-office allowance
A one-time home-office allowance of INR 15,000 is available after probation for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted under the reimbursement policy within 60 days of purchase.

## Working from another city or country
Working from a different city for up to 10 days a year needs manager approval. Working from another country is not permitted without written approval from HR, because of tax and legal implications.
