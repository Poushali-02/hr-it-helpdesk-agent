# Device Policy

## Laptop issuance
Every new employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Personal devices may not be used to store company source code or customer data.

## Loss, theft and damage
Report a lost or stolen device to it-helpdesk@northwind.example immediately, and within 4 hours at the latest. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. For theft, a police report (FIR) is also required. Accidental damage is covered once per device lifetime; repeated damage may be charged to the employee.

## Return on exit
All devices, chargers, access cards and accessories must be returned to IT on or before the last working day. Final settlement is released after IT confirms the return.

## Software
Only software approved by IT may be installed. Request new software through the IT helpdesk.
