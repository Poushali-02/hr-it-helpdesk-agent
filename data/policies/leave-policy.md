# Leave Policy

## Entitlements
Every full-time employee receives the following paid leave each calendar year:
- Casual leave: 12 days
- Sick leave: 10 days
- Earned leave: 15 days (accrued at 1.25 days per month)

## Carry-forward
Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December and cannot be carried forward. Unused sick leave also lapses. Up to 10 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year.

## Notice period and approval
Casual leave of up to 2 days needs 2 working days' notice. Casual leave of 3 or more consecutive days needs at least 5 working days' notice. Earned leave needs at least 10 working days' notice. Sick leave should be reported to your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
Requests are raised in the HR portal and approved by your reporting manager. Leave over 5 consecutive days also needs HR approval.

## Sick leave documentation
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
